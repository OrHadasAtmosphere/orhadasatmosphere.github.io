From individual storms
to the climate system.

I’m Or Hadas, an atmospheric scientist studying how storms, clouds, and large-scale circulation shape our climate.

I am a Hess Postdoctoral Fellow at Princeton University, working with Stephan Fueglistaler. I completed my PhD at the Weizmann Institute of Science with Yohai Kaspi.

Weather and climate across scales

What connects the life cycle of a single storm to the behavior of the climate system?

I combine theory, idealized models, reanalysis data, observations, and machine learning to understand the mechanisms behind midlatitude climate—from jet streams and storm growth to clouds, radiation, and the ocean.
