Weather and climate across scales

I’m Or Hadas, an atmospheric scientist studying how climate emerges from interactions across scales and among its different components.

I am a Hess Postdoctoral Fellow at Princeton University, working with Prof. Stephan Fueglistaler. I completed my PhD at the Weizmann Institute of Science with Prof. Yohai Kaspi.
